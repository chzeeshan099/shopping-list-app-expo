Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Todo App ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Todo App.

1. Information We Collect

Todo App does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

The app allows you to create and manage tasks, which may include:

  • Task titles
  • Task descriptions
  • Task priorities
  • Due dates
  • Task completion status
  • Important task status
  • Task history

2. Local Data Storage

Your task data is stored locally on your device. The app does not upload your tasks to our servers or store your task information in a remote database.

Because your task data is stored locally, the developer does not have access to the tasks you create and manage within the app.

3. Task Sharing

Todo App provides an optional task-sharing feature that allows you to share a task with other people.

When you choose to share a task, the app creates an image of the selected task design and uses your device's sharing functionality to allow you to choose an available sharing application.

You may share the task through applications such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, or other supported applications.

Once you choose a third-party application to share your task, the shared information may be processed according to that application's own privacy policy and terms of service.

4. Internet Usage

Todo App does not require an online account or backend service to create, manage, or store your tasks.

The app does not transmit your task data to our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Todo App does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or user-account services.

However, when you voluntarily share a task using another application, that third-party application may process the shared content according to its own privacy policy.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete your tasks directly from the app using the available delete options.

Since task data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data, depending on your device's operating system and settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Todo App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Security

Since task information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers, we do not have access to your locally stored task information.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and its stored data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
